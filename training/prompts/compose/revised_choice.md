# Choices revised with a reason

Someone talks through several decisions (what to order, which flight, which contractor, which colour, which plan) and revises some of them, giving the reason between the two options: "I want the blue one, except it's too expensive right now, so let's go with the red one instead". Some revisions are a new decision restated as a full clause ("carrots or actually no, let's go with peas"); some keep part of the first choice ("the blue for the kitchen, but the red for the hall"). The reason is content the cleanup keeps. The final choices must be unambiguous to a careful reader.

The cleanup keeps each final choice with its reason, and drops only the abandoned option.

Layout is "prose" unless the speaker is listing things to buy or do.
