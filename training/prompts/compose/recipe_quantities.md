# A recipe or cooking plan with quantity changes

Someone dictates a recipe, or a cooking plan for a meal, with many quantities, temperatures and times, and changes some as they go ("two cups of flour, no, two and a half", "bake at 350, uh actually 375 for this pan"). Some ingredients are optional or hedged ("a pinch of chili if you want"), some steps give a reason ("let it rest or it'll fall apart").

The cleanup is an ingredient list and numbered or listed steps with the final quantities, keeping every option, hedge and reason.

Layout is "list" or "mixed".

Tags: quantity_change, optional_item, reasoned_step, temperatures_and_times.
