# An email with a list and a late correction

Someone dictates an email or message to a named person: context, a list of things (items to bring, points to cover, dates to hold), questions for the recipient, and a sign-off. Near the end they correct something from an earlier sentence ("oh and in the part about the budget, make that 4,000 not 3,500"). They may say spoken punctuation or layout commands ("new paragraph", "comma").

The cleanup is the email with the correction applied where it belongs, questions kept as questions, and a list where the speaker listed things.

Layout is "mixed" or "prose".

Tags: email, late_correction, questions, spoken_layout.
