# A ramble across several topics

A long, loose dictation that moves across three or more topics: a story about the day, a plan for the weekend, an embedded list of things to buy or do, a question the speaker still needs answered, a reminder to themselves. They circle back to an earlier topic, add afterthoughts ("oh and"), and correct something from minutes before. At least one question must stay a question in the cleanup.

The cleanup gives paragraphs where the topic changes and a list only for the part that is a list.

Layout is "mixed".

Tags: topic_changes, embedded_list, open_question, circle_back.
