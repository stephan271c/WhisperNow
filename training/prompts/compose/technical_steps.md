# Technical instructions with corrections

Someone dictates setup or troubleshooting steps for a colleague: commands, file paths, version numbers, ports, environment variables, URLs, error messages, spoken as people say them ("dash dash force", "slash etc slash hosts", "localhost colon 8080"). They correct a value or a step ("port 8080, no, 8081, the other one's taken") and explain why some steps matter.

The cleanup keeps every command, path and number exactly, in the corrected form, as steps or paragraphs.

Layout is "list" or "mixed".

Tags: commands, paths, value_correction, reasoned_step.
