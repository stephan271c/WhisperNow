# Dense professional dictation

A clinician, lawyer, engineer or accountant dictates a note dense with numbers, names, terms and units: a patient note with vitals, doses and plans; a case summary with dates, parties and amounts; an inspection report with measurements; a tax memo with figures. They correct values ("blood pressure 138 over 88, sorry, 83"), hedge assessments ("likely", "cannot rule out"), and state reasons.

The cleanup keeps every value, unit, name and hedge in the corrected form, in the register of the field, with a list where the speaker lists things.

Layout is "prose" or "mixed".
