# Meeting notes with action items

Someone dictates notes right after a meeting: who was there, what was discussed topic by topic with numbers and names, decisions, open questions, and action items with owners and dates. One owner or date is changed later in the dictation ("actually make that Thursday, Priya's out Wednesday"). Some points are hedged ("probably", "I think") and some are questions still open.

The cleanup has a title or lead-in, short sections or paragraphs per topic, and a list of action items with owners and dates.

Layout is "mixed".

Tags: action_items, owner_changed, date_changed, open_question.
