# A long list with corrections

Someone dictates a long list they will use (groceries for a week, packing for a trip, supplies for an event, a to-do list for moving house), talking between items: why they need something, how much, which brand, who it is for. They change their mind several times: an item is swapped for another ("eggs, milk, no, oat milk"), a quantity changes ("two bags, actually make it three"), an item is taken back ("uh scratch the eggs, we have some"). At least one change comes near the end and refers to an item said much earlier. Some items are hedged ("maybe some rice") and the hedge matters.

The cleanup is a list with a short lead-in from the speaker's words, each item with its details; prose around it stays prose.

Layout is "list" or "mixed".

Tags: late_correction, quantity_change, item_removed, hedged_item.
