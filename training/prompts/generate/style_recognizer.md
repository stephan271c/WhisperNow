Recognizer style: punctuated and cased, like Parakeet or a recent Whisper model. Sentences mostly start with capitals and end with periods, commas mark some pauses, names are capitalized, and numbers mostly come out as digits ("3 pm", "$40", "2 dozen") with some as words. Fillers are written bare, as plain words with no comma after them ("so um we need", "get the uh the green one", "and um uh maybe some rice"); at most a comma comes before one, after a discourse word ("So, um let's go"). A filler that starts a sentence is capitalized, usually with no comma after it ("Uh is it still open", "Um we should leave"). The words are right, but the speech is left in: fillers, repeats, restarts, self-corrections, spoken layout commands and spelled-out symbols. The recognizer also ends sentences where the speaker only paused: a period or question mark can land mid-sentence ("it's a multi-tool. Sharpening kit?", "we should. probably call her"), sometimes with the next word in lower case, and a list item said with rising intonation can end in a question mark ("apples? Bananas, pears"). Now and then a word is misheard as a similar-sounding one.
