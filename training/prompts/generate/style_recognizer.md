Recognizer style: punctuated and cased, like Parakeet or a recent Whisper model. Sentences mostly start with capitals and end with periods, commas mark pauses, names are capitalized, and numbers mostly come out as digits ("3 pm", "$40", "2 dozen") with some as words. Fillers are written as words with commas around them ("Um, so we need", "and, uh, the"). The words are right, but the speech is left in: fillers, repeats, false starts, self-corrections, spoken layout commands and spelled-out symbols. Now and then a segment join puts a period mid-sentence ("we should. Probably call her") or a word is misheard as a similar-sounding one.
