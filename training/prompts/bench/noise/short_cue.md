# A correction with one short cue

The speaker says a value and corrects it at once with one short cue: "Friday, sorry, Saturday", "two, no, three boxes", "at four, I mean five". The value is a number, day, time, name, place, colour or item. One cue per correction, never a stack ("no wait sorry I mean"). One or two corrections per transcript, and everything else stays as said.

Evidence: each correction exactly as written, such as "Friday, sorry, Saturday".
