# Punctuation where the speaker paused

The recognizer put a period or question mark where the speaker only paused mid-sentence: "it's in the. second drawer", "we need about 25. Percent more", "the meeting was. moved to Friday". A list item said with rising intonation ends in "?": "garlic? Onions, spinach". At least two per transcript, with the word after a mid-sentence period in lowercase at least once. Recognizer style only.

Evidence: each split, exactly as written, such as "the. second drawer".
