# A restart with no cue

The speaker abandons a phrase and starts it again, with no cue word: "call them about I need to call them about the invoice", "if we can if the room is free we'll meet there". Sometimes a short stray fragment is left: "she tomorrow she's off". No "sorry", "I mean", "wait" or "let me rephrase".

Evidence: the abandoned phrase and its restart, exactly as written, such as "call them about I need to call them about".
