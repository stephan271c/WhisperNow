# Thinking aloud and mic checks

The speaker thinks aloud ("let's see", "and now let's see", "let me think", "what else", "hmm, where was I", "so yeah, I don't know") or checks the mic before starting ("Hello? Can you hear me?", "testing, testing", "is this on?", "okay, is it recording?"). About half the transcripts have a mic check, half think aloud, and some both. The rest is ordinary dictated content.

Evidence: each thinking-aloud phrase or mic check exactly as written.
