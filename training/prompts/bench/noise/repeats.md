# Doubled and tripled words

Words said twice or three times without meaning it: a pronoun or auxiliary ("we we", "it's it's", "is is"), a clause opener repeated ("I don't I don't know", "we should we should ask"), a conjunction tripled ("and and and"). At least two per transcript. Never a repeat that carries meaning ("very very", "that that").

Evidence: each repeat exactly as written, such as "it's it's".
