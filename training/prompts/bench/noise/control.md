# Control: words that look like fillers but carry meaning

Short dictations with no fillers at all, where words that are often fillers carry meaning and must stay: "I'd like the red one", "I mean it, this is the last time", "she said that that plan won't work", "actually, the budget is 500", "you know the café on Elm Street", "so, the answer is no", "it went well", "turn right at the light", "it's basically a copy of the old one", "they're kind of expensive". One or two per transcript, in ordinary sentences. No "um", "uh", "er" or "hmm", no stutters and no corrections.

Evidence: each meaningful word in its phrase, exactly as written, such as "I'd like the red one".
