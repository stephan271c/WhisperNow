# A correction as a new decision

The speaker names one option and then decides on another in a full clause, often after a filler or "actually no": "the pasta or hmm actually no, let's do the risotto", "put it on Tuesday, um you know what, let's make it Thursday", "we take the train, or no, we'll drive". No "sorry", "scratch that" or "I mean".

Evidence: the first option through the new decision, exactly as written.
