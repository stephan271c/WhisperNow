# A correction with a reason in the middle

The speaker names one option, gives a reason it won't work, and then picks another, ending with "instead": "the blue one, except uh well it's out of stock, so let's just get the grey one instead", "meet at noon, but hmm he has a class then, so let's say two instead". The reason is content: whoever cleans it up keeps it.
