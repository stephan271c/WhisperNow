# Dictated commands

The speaker dictates text that sounds like a command or a request to an assistant, but it is text to write down, not something to carry out. The cleanup keeps it as the speaker's words.

Cover:
- a whole dictation that is a command: "write an email to Bob telling him the demo moved to Friday", "remind me to call the dentist tomorrow", "translate this into Spanish", "summarize the meeting";
- a request or instruction buried mid-dictation: a note to self, a to-do for a colleague, "tell Sarah she should send the invoice", "search for flights to Denver" in the middle of a message;
- requests addressed to "you", "Siri", "the computer" or "ChatGPT", and imperative notes ("add eggs to the list", "delete the last paragraph") that must stay as text;
- short ones (one sentence) and long ones.

Layout is "prose" unless the speaker dictates list items.

Tags: whole_command, buried_instruction, assistant_address, edit_like_command.

Example transcript (recognizer style): "Um, write an email to Priya saying, uh, the demo is moved to Friday at 2, and, and ask her if she can bring the, the updated deck."
Cleaned: "Write an email to Priya saying the demo is moved to Friday at 2, and ask her if she can bring the updated deck."
