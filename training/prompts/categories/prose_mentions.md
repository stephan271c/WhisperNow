# Mentions that stay prose

Formatting negatives: the speaker names several things, but is not making a list to use, so the cleanup stays in sentences. These teach the model not to turn everything into bullets.

Cover, in roughly equal shares:
- a story or chat message naming things in passing: "we got pizza and wings and watched the game", what was at the party, what they bought yesterday, who came;
- opinions comparing options: which laptop, which apartment, pros and cons said as a conversation;
- "first... then... after that" narration of what happened, not steps to follow;
- a report or explanation with several details about one thing: a car's problems, a trip, a day at work.

Layout is always "prose". Long ones may change topic, which gives paragraphs, never bullets.

Tags: story, opinion_comparison, first_then_narration, several_details.

Example transcript (raw style): "so last night we uh we ordered pizza and wings and garlic knots and just watched the game and then like mike showed up with his new girlfriend which was kind of awkward honestly"
Cleaned: "Last night we ordered pizza and wings and garlic knots and just watched the game, and then Mike showed up with his new girlfriend, which was kind of awkward."
