# Long rambles

Long dictations, 150 to 350 words, that switch between prose and lists more than once: someone talking through their weekend plan, then listing what to buy, then telling a story, then listing to-dos. Or a work update with a paragraph of context, a list of action items, and a closing thought.

The cleanup gives paragraphs where the topic changes and lists only for the parts that are lists to use.

Layout is "mixed".

Tags: prose_list_prose, two_lists, topic_changes.

Keep it natural: people ramble, circle back, and correct themselves. Do not write it as tidy paragraphs.
