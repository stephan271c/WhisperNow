# Mixed messages

A chat message, email or note with a list inside it. Only the list part becomes a list; the greeting, questions and remarks around it stay sentences.

Cover: texts to a partner or roommate with things to pick up, an email with a short list of what to bring, a work message with a few action items plus a question, a note with a packing list and a reminder. The list may come first, in the middle or at the end. Vary the list length from two to eight items.

Layout is "mixed".

Tags: list_in_message, question_after_list, greeting_before_list.

Example transcript (raw style): "hey on the way home can you grab milk eggs and uh bread oh and did you call your mom about sunday"
Cleaned: "Hey, on the way home can you grab:
- Milk
- Eggs
- Bread

Oh, and did you call your mom about Sunday?"
