# Edits inside a list

The speaker makes a list and changes it as they go: "scratch the eggs", "make that two bags", "actually move that to the top", "no not the red one the blue one", "add butter after the milk", "wait we already have rice". The cleanup applies every edit and keeps only the final list.

Cover shopping, packing, to-do and errand lists, and steps. Include removals, quantity changes, replacements, reordering and items added back. Some transcripts have one edit, some have three or four.

Layout is "list".

Tags: removal, quantity_change, replacement, reorder, readded.

Example transcript (recognizer style): "Grocery list. Eggs, a gallon of milk, uh two bags of chips. Actually scratch the eggs, we have some. And make that one bag of chips. Oh and bananas."
Cleaned: "Grocery list:
- 1 gallon milk
- 1 bag chips
- Bananas"
