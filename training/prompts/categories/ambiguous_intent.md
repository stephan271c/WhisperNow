# Ambiguous intent

Dictations where it is not obvious whether a list is wanted: two items in a text to a friend ("can you grab milk and eggs"), a to-do said as a sentence in a message, three errands mentioned while chatting, a short note with two things to remember. Either layout could be defended; the checker accepts a reasonable choice and rejects a clearly wrong one.

Make them short to medium, casual, and varied. Give your own best guess in "layout".

Tags: two_items, errands_in_chat, note_to_self.
